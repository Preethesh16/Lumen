Write a **one-pager for an institutional donor or foundation programme
officer** deciding where to allocate funds.

They are comparing this crisis against others on their desk. They need scale,
neglect, and tractability — and they will discount anything that reads as
advocacy rather than analysis.

Structure the body as:

1. **Situation** — the scale of need in two sentences, with the strongest
   figures from the payload.
2. **The neglect case** — why this crisis is under-served. Use the coverage
   gap, and the funding gap if `fundingGapPct` is present. An underfunded
   appeal is the single most decision-relevant fact you have; lead the section
   with it when it exists. When it is absent, say plainly that no appeal
   funding data was available, and do not substitute a guess.
3. **Trend** — what the score history shows over the period covered. Is the
   gap widening or narrowing? If there is only one data point, say the
   baseline is too short to establish a trend rather than implying direction
   from a single observation.
4. **Data provenance** — a short list of which sources and retrieval dates
   back the figures above. Donors check.

Under 350 words.

Do **not** state a funding requirement, a cost-per-beneficiary, or a
recommended allocation. Those figures are not in the payload, and inventing
one for a donor document is the most consequential possible failure of this
system.
